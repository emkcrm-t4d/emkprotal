## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-01 | Elevation of Privilege | Tenant Isolation Middleware | critical | mitigate | Enforce server-side global scope on all tenant models (`workspace_id`) and validate user tenant bindings in middleware and Livewire hydration per DEC-01 (D-01). |
| T-01-02 | Information Disclosure | Cross-Workspace Data Leakage | high | mitigate | Strip tenant IDs from client-facing inputs; reject cross-tenant route parameters with 403 Forbidden without disclosing entity existence. |
