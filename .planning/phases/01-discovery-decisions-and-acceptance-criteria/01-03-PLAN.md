    1. Role Archetypes & Scopes:
       - `superadmin`: Global scope across all workspaces; full system configuration; backup and restore operations; hard deletions; immutable audit log viewer; cross-workspace reporting; manages all modules.
       - `global_admin`: Global scope across all workspaces; user and role provisioning; restriction and revocation of memberships with management instruction; cannot modify platform secrets or audit history.
       - `workspace_manager`: Workspace-scoped to assigned workspace (`ws-emk-dhaka`); full administration of leads, members, forms, and invoices within workspace; verification of bKash payments per DEC-05 (D-05); approval and conversion of leads per DEC-03 (D-03); conversion reversal per DEC-04 (D-04); approval of member email change requests per DEC-07 (D-07); delegates authorized module actions to workspace staff; strictly blocked from viewing or discovering other workspaces per DEC-01 (D-01).

Prohibited from conversion reversal, email change approval, or user role delegation.
       - `member`: Individual portal scope; strictly read-only access to own profile, documents, and communication threads per DEC-06 (D-06); submit profile change requests; submit dedicated email change requests; submit renewal requests. Prohibited from direct profile edits, cancellation, or attendance logging.

       - `members`: `view`, `edit`, `restrict`, `revoke`, `export_csv`, `export_excel`.
       - `member_portal`: `view_profile`, `request_change`, `request_email_change`, `request_renewal`.
       - `invoices`: `view`, `generate`, `verify_payment`, `export`.
       - `dashboard_reports`: `view_dashboard`, `generate_reports`, `export_metrics`.
       - `platform_services`: `manage_settings`, `manage_users`, `delegate_roles`, `view_audit_logs`, `manage_backups`.
       - `migration`: `import_zoho`, `export_zoho`, `reconcile_data`.

       - 2D Markdown Matrix mapping every `{module}.{action}` against the 5 roles (`superadmin`, `global_admin`, `workspace_manager`, `delegated_staff`, `member`).

    Detail the following 8 comprehensive scenarios:
    1. Scenario 1: Public 31-Field Form Submission & Validation
       - Given an unauthenticated applicant visiting the EMK Center Dhaka membership form.
       - When they enter their date of birth indicating age &lt; 18, Then Field 7 (Consent Form Upload) becomes mandatory per DEC-09 (D-09).
       - When they enter a valid 10-character uppercase bKash TxID and upload compliant documents and studio photo, Then submission creates a Lead record in `ws-emk-dhaka` and dispatches a confirmation email.
       - When an applicant submits an already registered email or duplicate bKash TxID, Then submission is rejected with an inline error message per DEC-05 (D-05).
    2. Scenario 2: Independent Tri-State Lead Verification
       - Given a Lead in `submitted` status in `ws-emk-dhaka`.
       - When staff reviews the bKash TxID and verifies payment, Payment stream transitions to `verified`.
       - When staff detects an unreadable ID document, Document stream transitions to `missing_items` and an official email is triggered to applicant requesting re-upload.
       - When applicant re-uploads valid document and studio photo passes the 5-point checklist, Document and Photo streams transition to `verified`.
    3. Scenario 3: Atomic Lead-to-Member Conversion
       - Given a Lead where Payment, Document, and Photo verification streams are all `verified`.
       - When Workspace Manager clicks "Convert to Member", Then within an atomic transaction: Member profile is created, Member ID `{SPACE_CODE}-{YEAR}-{SEQ}` assigned, Start Date set to Approval Date per DEC-08 (D-08), End Date calculated dynamically from selected term, Post-Conversion Invoice generated, documents re-associated, and welcome email queued.
       - Given any verification stream is NOT `verified`, When conversion is attempted, Then the action is blocked server-side.
    4. Scenario 4: Controlled Conversion Reversal
       - Given an erroneously converted Member within 30 days of conversion.
       - When Workspace Manager provides a mandatory reason (&gt;= 20 chars) and confirms reversal, Then Member status transitions to `archived_reversed`, invoices voided, Lead status reverted to `under_review`, portal login disabled, and an immutable audit event recorded per DEC-04 (D-04).
    5. Scenario 5: Multi-Workspace Tenancy Boundary Defense
       - Given a Workspace Manager authenticated in `ws-emk-dhaka`.
       - When they attempt to navigate to another workspace's leads or modify URL/Livewire request parameters to target another workspace ID, Then the system returns 403 Forbidden and records a security audit log event per DEC-01 (D-01).
    6. Scenario 6: Dedicated Member Email Change Workflow
       - Given an active Member logged into the Member Portal.
       - When they request an email update, Then they cannot edit email directly; a formal request is submitted, appears in the Workspace Manager panel, and dispatches an alert email to the Manager per DEC-07 (D-07).
       - When the Manager approves the request, Then the Member login email is updated, previous email archived, and an audit event logged.
    7. Scenario 7: Read-Only Member Portal Access
       - Given a newly approved Member.
       - When they log in for the first time, Then they are forced to change their password per DEC-06 (D-06).
       - When viewing profile screens, Then all personal details, uploaded documents, and communication threads are displayed in read-only mode, while internal staff notes, verification states, and audit trails remain strictly hidden.
    8. Scenario 8: Membership Expiry and Renewal Flow
       - Given a Member whose membership expires in 30 days.
       - When the renewal window opens, Then the Member Portal displays a renewal banner and manual bKash submission form.
       - When Member submits bKash TxID and staff verifies payment, Then new term is appended and invoice generated without requiring a new 31-field application form.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Manager Role Delegation → Staff Permissions | Workspace Manager granting elevated permissions to staff |
| Member Portal → Internal Notes and Data | Member session requesting internal staff notes, verification states, or invoices |
| Backup Storage → Infrastructure Operators | Access to encrypted database and document backups in offsite storage |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-07 | Elevation of Privilege | Staff Role Delegation | high | mitigate | Hardcode non-delegable actions (`reverse_conversion`, `manage_users`) in delegation service so Managers cannot assign forbidden permissions to staff per DELEGATION-MATRIX.md. |
| T-01-08 | Information Disclosure | Portal Internal Data Leakage | critical | mitigate | Explicitly exclude internal notes, verification checklists, and audit events from member portal Eloquent resources and Livewire components per DEC-06 (D-06). |
| T-01-09 | Repudiation | Unaudited Data Operations | high | mitigate | Mandate append-only audit event logging on all exports, conversions, reversals, and deletions with actor identity and timestamp per DEC-11 (D-11). |
| T-01-SC | Tampering | Package Manager Dependencies | high | mitigate | No package installs in Phase 1 discovery; software dependencies deferred to Phase 3 foundation with strict package legitimacy gates. |
</threat_model>
